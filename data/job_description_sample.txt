Job Title: Machine Learning Engineer (NLP Focus)

Company: TechNova Solutions
Location: Bangalore, India (Hybrid)

Job Description:
We are looking for a skilled Machine Learning Engineer with a strong focus
on Natural Language Processing to join our AI team. The ideal candidate
will design, build, and deploy NLP-based systems that solve real business
problems, including text classification, information extraction, and
semantic search.

Responsibilities:
- Develop and fine-tune NLP models for text classification and ranking
- Build data pipelines for cleaning and preprocessing large text datasets
- Apply machine learning and deep learning techniques to real-world problems
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deploy models into production
- Evaluate model performance and iterate based on metrics

Required Skills:
- Strong proficiency in Python
- Hands-on experience with Machine Learning and Natural Language Processing
- Experience with scikit-learn, NLTK or spaCy, and TensorFlow or PyTorch
- Familiarity with data analysis using Pandas and NumPy
- Experience building REST APIs with Flask or FastAPI
- Working knowledge of SQL and databases such as MySQL or MongoDB
- Experience with version control (Git) and cloud platforms (AWS or GCP)
- Strong problem-solving skills and ability to work in an agile team

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with Streamlit or similar tools for building ML demos
- Exposure to deep learning frameworks such as Keras or Hugging Face Transformers
- Good communication and teamwork skills
